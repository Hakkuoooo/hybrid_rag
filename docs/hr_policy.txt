NEXUS TECH - HR POLICY DOCUMENT

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
All full-time employees are entitled to 28 days annual leave per year including UK public holidays. Leave must be requested via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried over to the following year, but not beyond March 31st.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 10 days fully paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required.

3. EXPENSES
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days. Claims above £500 require pre-approval from a department head. Receipts required for all claims above £25. Reimbursed within 15 working days.

4. TRAINING BUDGET
Each employee receives £1,500 annual learning budget for courses, conferences, or certifications. If you leave within 6 months of a company-funded course over £500, you repay 50%.

5. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary carers get 52 weeks, first 26 at full pay. Secondary carers get 4 weeks full pay. Request at least 8 weeks before start date.
